How to play

Glitch-bots are invading the Starlight Arena! Blast them, survive each wave, and pick an upgrade after every wave. Every 5th wave a giant Boss Core shows up.

Controls

WASD move  ·  Mouse aim + hold click to shoot
or ↑↓←→ shoot in that direction (keyboard-only)
Space / Shift / right-click: dash (invincible while dashing)
F toggle auto-aim (great on a trackpad)  ·  M mute  ·  P pause (solo)
123 pick an upgrade

Touch: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to aim (auto-aim is on otherwise), tap the DASH button.

Co-op

One person presses Host Co-op Game and gets a 4-letter room code. Friends open the same game on their own device, type the code and press Join. Up to 4 pilots.

If a teammate gets knocked down, fly next to them and stay close to revive them. If the whole squad goes down it's game over. Downed pilots also come back at the end of each wave.

Collect stars for points and hearts to heal.
